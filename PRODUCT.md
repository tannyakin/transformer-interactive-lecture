# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Students in the AI UniPod LLM lecture series (University of Lagos), working through the material on their own after class. They read on laptops in the lab or at home, and often on phones in between. Most are comfortable with basic linear algebra but meeting transformers for the first time. Their job: actually understand each idea well enough to redo the calculation by hand, not just recognise the words.

## Product Purpose

One interactive page per lecture that follows the lecture notes in order and turns every worked example into a step by step walkthrough that plays at reading pace, can be stepped by hand, and stops to ask the learner to predict at the moments that matter. Success is a student who closes the page able to compute ALiBi weights or a KV cache size with a calculator and explain why the numbers came out that way, and who can come back later to exactly where they stopped.

## Brand Personality

Patient, precise, curious. It behaves like a good tutor sitting beside you: never rushes, never hand-waves the maths, and invites you to poke at things. The voice is plain and warm, talks to "you", explains why before what, and never uses marketing language. Every number shown is exact and checkable.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages: gradient heroes, big stat numbers, identical icon card grids, a card around every section.
- Old LMS pages (Moodle and similar): grey boxes, tiny text, clutter, intranet feel.
- Gamified quiz apps: points, streaks, confetti, loud badges that pull attention away from the ideas.
- Dense academic PDFs: walls of text and formulas with nothing to see move and no room to breathe.

## Design Principles

1. The maths is the hero. Numbers, formulas and diagrams get the best typography and the most space; chrome stays quiet until needed.
2. Show the computation moving. Every calculation is watched step by step, never dumped as a finished block.
3. Predict before reveal. At key moments the learner commits to a guess before seeing the answer; feedback explains why, never scores.
4. Follow the notes' order. Each part answers one question and hands off to the next, exactly as the lecture flows.
5. Respect the reader's pace. Autoplay waits long enough to read, stops when asked, and remembers where the learner left off.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.2 AA contrast in both light and dark themes.
- Full keyboard use: arrows step walkthroughs, every control is a real button with a label.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` turns autoplay and count-up animation off and leaves a plain stepper.
- Colour is never the only signal: negatives carry a minus sign, highlights also change weight or outline.
- Works on a 375px phone with no horizontal page scroll, and when opened straight from disk.
